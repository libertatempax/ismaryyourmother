The missing proof

Verses often quoted assume she is our Mother. They don't prove it.

Most Catholics already believe Mary is their Mother. Asked to show it from Scripture, they reach for the same two verses.

Behold, your mother!John 19:27Given to one disciple
Do whatever he tells you.John 2:5Spoken to servants at a wedding

To someone who already believes, these verses confirm it. To someone who doesn't, they hold only if you first grant that Mary is our Mother, and any argument that has not proven its premises first is logically invalid. It loses the argument even if it is true, and gives an excuse to reject the truth along with the argument.

This book starts where the proof starts: on the first page of the Bible, with a promise God makes to the Serpent.

Genesis 3:15

Four ways to read one verse

"I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your seed and her seed; he shall bruise your head, and you shall bruise his heel." Read it four ways, and every reading arrives at the same Woman.

1
As it is written

Two sides, and you belong to one

God starts the war Himself: a Woman and her children against the Serpent and his. The two sides are two families of real people.

2
Inside its scene

The Woman cannot be Eve

Eve had just taken the Serpent's side. If the Woman were Eve, there would be no sides, no war, no choice, and no real Savior.

3
As the story unfolds

The Mother every generation waited for

Abraham's seed, David's seed, Isaiah's virgin who conceives a son. One Woman is asked, and she says Yes.

4
From the end

"The rest of her seed"

In Revelation 12, John calls every Christian The Woman's seed, using the same Greek word as Genesis 3:15. The Woman whose Son crushed the Serpent's head is Mary.

Every reading ends at Mary, and her children are real people.
